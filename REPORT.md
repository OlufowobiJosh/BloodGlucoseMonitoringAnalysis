# Descriptive Analysis — Portfolio Demonstration

**Context:** Excel-based healthcare monitoring analysis, recreated with synthetic data for public demonstration.

**Problem:** Raw monitoring entries may be scattered across dates, readings and free text comments, making it difficult to review trends and recording patterns consistently.

**Approach:** Standardise data fields; distinguish recorded results from declined occasions; categorise readings using the project-specific example thresholds; group monitoring comments by time of day; and present counts, averages, trends and distributions in an Excel dashboard.

**Deliverable:** A dark-navy, editable Excel dashboard with KPI cards, four charts and a single year dropdown controlling all chart calculations. The original workplace analysis was presented to a manager; the public demonstration does not reproduce confidential outcomes.

**Interpretation:** The sample charts illustrate how descriptive reporting can surface patterns that warrant review, but the generated figures must not be interpreted as evidence of real patient outcomes.

**Confidentiality:** Every record and result in this portfolio version is synthetic. No clinical records or original internal screenshots are included.
